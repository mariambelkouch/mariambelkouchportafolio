01 — The thesis

Organizations are designed,
whether intentionally or not.

When organizations become more complex, the symptoms often surface as “People problems”: founders become bottlenecks, teams stop taking ownership, managers escalate decisions, and good people compensate for bad processes.

I look for the design problem underneath the symptom — then build the system that allows people to operate without compensating for it.

Complexity is inevitable.
Ambiguity is optional.

06 — The question I start with

I don't start by asking
what People problem needs fixing.

I start by asking what the organization is trying to accomplish, where the system is creating friction, and what needs to be redesigned so the people inside it can do their jobs without compensating for the design.
